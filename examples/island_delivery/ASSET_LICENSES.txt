ISLAND AIR VISUAL ASSETS
=======================
Assets are downloaded and SHA256-checked by cmake/IslandDeliveryAssets.cmake.
Downloads stay in the build cache. Existing Kenney character models are reused from assets/racer/crowd.
Keep this file, AUDIO_LICENSE.txt and Cesium-LICENSE.md with redistributed builds.

aircraft.glb: Cesium Air, Copyright 2011-2026 CesiumJS Contributors.
Apache License 2.0; complete upstream license is downloaded as Cesium-LICENSE.md.
https://github.com/CesiumGS/cesium/tree/85500087b890479ffa3bf0b4cf0db55887c2d89a/Apps/SampleData/models/CesiumAir
The original GLB is unchanged. Runtime placement scales and rotates the model;
the two original propeller nodes are animated by the game.

These original images are downloaded unmodified and renamed by the manifest.
Terrain, airport structures, palms and markings are generated in C++ game code.

Airport vehicles: Kenney Racing Kit, CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://github.com/KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-Racing/tree/2f2e5f2646dda89cb21d4e8539bab60c6e955dc8/models
Two original truck models and their palette are downloaded unchanged.
Airport people: Kenney Mini Characters, CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-characters
The existing rigid, joint-separated versions in assets/racer/crowd are copied
into the build cache. The game animates their arms and legs through scene nodes.
