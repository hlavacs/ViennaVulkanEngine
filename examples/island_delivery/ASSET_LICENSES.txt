ISLAND AIR VISUAL ASSETS
=======================
Assets are downloaded and SHA256-checked by cmake/IslandDeliveryAssets.cmake.
No external photographs, models or recordings are stored in this source tree.
Keep this file, AUDIO_LICENSE.txt and Cesium-LICENSE.md with redistributed builds.

aircraft.glb: Cesium Air, Copyright 2011-2026 CesiumJS Contributors.
Apache License 2.0; complete upstream license is downloaded as Cesium-LICENSE.md.
https://github.com/CesiumGS/cesium/tree/85500087b890479ffa3bf0b4cf0db55887c2d89a/Apps/SampleData/models/CesiumAir
The original GLB is unchanged. Runtime placement scales and rotates the model;
the two original propeller nodes are animated by the game.
